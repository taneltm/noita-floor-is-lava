# Floor is lava

You can stand on physics objects, but touch the floor for too long and it turns to lava.

This vibecoded mod is easy for the professional tablet surfers, right?

## Rules

- 10 seconds of grace at the start of a run.
- At the start of each run, you'll find a water potion and an awkwardly shaped stone to stand on.
- Standing or walking on terrain or powder for a quarter of a second turns the ground around your feet into lava, like a Touch of spell. Red sparks at your feet warn you the moment you touch down.
- The patch has a 30 px radius at 100 max HP, plus 1 px for every max HP point above that.
- After each patch there's a 1-second cooldown before the timer starts again.
- Liquids don't count. If you're standing in water, the floor under it is what counts.
- It's up to you to discover where you can stand.
- Jumping, levitating or swimming without touching the ground resets the timer.

## Settings

- **Hardcore**: instead of a patch under your feet, every pixel of the material you stood on turns into lava, everywhere in the world. Holy Mountain brickwork still only gets the patch, so the mountains survive, but break enough of one and Steve will come for you. Turning rock or sand into lava can bring down big parts of the world. Takes effect on a new run.
- **Debug info**: shows the grace countdown, ground timer, cooldown and the last material read in the top left.

Both are off by default.

## Install

Copy the `floor_is_lava` folder into `Noita/mods/`, enable it in the Mods menu and start a new game. Keep the folder name as `floor_is_lava`.
